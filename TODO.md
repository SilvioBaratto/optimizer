This project is an end-to-end optimizer.

There are many layers that make it up:

A scheduler whose sole and exclusive job every morning is to download the data. This component must be stable, failure-proof, fast, easily scalable and lightweight — it is the heart of everything and its only job is downloading the data.

I need to build a team of AIs that simulate an investment fund. Four AIs: one studies the economy, one decides where to put the money, one controls risk, one executes. They work together, they talk to each other, and in the end they suggest which stocks to buy and in what percentage.

A frontend that is nothing other than a true optimizer, with a level of detail worthy of the best investment fund.

An API that communicates with the frontend to handle all the frontend's requests.
